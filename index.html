Kinder Learning Academy is a neighborhood child care center and preschool on Baseline Road in Laveen. We believe children deserve to be happy, healthy and safe, and we build every day around that promise: a safe, enriched learning environment where curiosity is encouraged and every child is known by name.

Families can choose the learning style that fits their child best: a traditional preschool classroom or a Montessori approach. Both are guided by The Creative Curriculum, which gives children of every age the chance to learn through discovery and play, build lasting friendships, and grow in expressing and managing their feelings.

Our care doesn't stop when the school bell rings. We offer transportation to and from school, homework assistance and after-school activities for children up to 12 years old, plus breakfast, lunch and an afternoon snack every day. Financial aid is available for families who qualify.

Center Programs

Each classroom is designed around one stage of growth, so your child learns with peers at the same age. Select an age group to see what it offers.

Infant (4 Weeks – 18 Months)

We welcome babies as young as 4 weeks old into a calm, cozy room where the same caregivers greet them every day. Each baby keeps their own rhythm for bottles, naps and play, and parents get updates on the little moments that matter.

What we focus on:

  • Steady, loving bonds with familiar caregivers
  • Feeding and nap routines that follow your baby's cues
  • Tummy time, reaching, rolling and first steps
  • Songs, books and back-and-forth "conversations"
Ask about infant openings →

Toddler (18 Months – 3 Years)

Toddlers are ready to explore everything, and our classroom gives them the room to do it safely. With Montessori-style shelves and hands-on materials, they practice doing things on their own while making their very first friends.

What we focus on:

  • Self-care skills, from washing hands to potty learning
  • New words every day through stories and songs
  • Sensory bins, art and building play
  • Taking turns and naming big feelings
Ask about toddler openings →

Preschool (3 – 4 Years)

Choose a traditional preschool classroom or our Montessori approach. Either way, The Creative Curriculum guides the day, so children learn through discovery and play, build friendships, and grow in expressing and managing emotions.

What we focus on:

  • Letters, sounds, numbers and patterns
  • Art, music and pretend play
  • Solving problems with friends in small groups
  • Confidence with classroom routines
Ask about preschool openings →

Pre-K (4 – 5 Years)

The year before kindergarten is all about getting ready. Pre-K students strengthen early reading, writing and math, take on hands-on projects, and practice the focus and independence that make the move to "big school" feel easy.

What we focus on:

  • Early reading, writing and number sense
  • Science and building projects
  • Following directions and staying on task
  • Kindness, teamwork and leadership
Ask about Pre-K openings →

Before & After School (5 – 12 Years)

Before and after class, school-age children can relax, get homework help and join after-school activities with friends, all with caring teachers close by.

Our Philosophy

At Kinder Learning Academy, we strive to nurture each child's unique qualities and potential. No two children grow the same way or on the same schedule. Like the sun and the moon, each one shines in their own time, so we never compare one child to another.

We are proud to be an inclusive school. We value diversity and the unique contributions every child brings to the classroom, and we believe that when children learn side by side, everyone grows. Our goal is simple: give your child what they need to laugh, be challenged and feel delighted, and give you peace of mind.
