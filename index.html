Welcome to Kinder Academy

A place where every child gets to grow at their own pace

Kinder Academy is a locally owned child care center and preschool on Baseline Road in Laveen. Our brand-new building was designed around how young children actually learn: sunlit classrooms, cozy reading nooks, low shelves that invite independence, and a shaded playground made for running, climbing and imagining.

We believe every child is smart in their own way. Some light up with music, some with numbers, others with movement, building, or caring for friends. Our teachers watch closely, celebrate those strengths, and plan play-based experiences that build confidence, curiosity and kindness.

We are proud to be an inclusive school that values diversity and the unique contributions every child brings to the classroom. When children learn together, everyone benefits.

Center programs

Programs for every stage, from first steps to homework help

Each classroom is designed for a specific stage of development. Choose a program to see what a day looks like.

4 weeks – 18 months

Infant Program

Gentle, responsive care in a calm room built for our youngest learners. Your baby follows their own feeding and nap schedule, and our caregivers send home daily notes so you never miss a milestone.

Key focus areas

  • Warm, consistent bonds with primary caregivers
  • Individual feeding, sleep and diapering routines
  • Tummy time, sensory play and early movement
  • Songs, stories and first words every day
Ask about infant openings

18 months – 3 years

Toddler Program

Toddlers want to do it themselves, and we let them. Hands-on centers, music and movement, and plenty of outdoor time help busy toddlers build words, confidence and early friendships.

Key focus areas

  • Self-help skills: feeding, dressing and potty training
  • Language growth through songs, books and conversation
  • Sensory, art and building exploration
  • Sharing, turn-taking and naming feelings
Ask about toddler openings

3 – 4 years

Preschool Program

Curiosity takes the lead. Through themed units, dramatic play, art and science centers, preschoolers build early literacy and math skills while learning how to be a good friend.

Key focus areas

  • Letters, sounds, counting and patterns
  • Creative expression through art, music and pretend play
  • Problem-solving and working in small groups
  • Independence and classroom routines
Ask about preschool openings

4 – 5 years

Pre-K Program

Our kindergarten-readiness year. Children practice early reading and writing, explore hands-on STEM projects, and build the focus and self-control they'll need for a confident first day of kindergarten.

Key focus areas

  • Pre-reading, writing and number sense
  • Hands-on science, technology, engineering & math
  • Self-regulation and following multi-step directions
  • Leadership, empathy and teamwork
Ask about Pre-K openings

5 – 12 years

Before & After School

A safe, fun place to start and end the school day. School-age friends get a healthy snack, homework time with support, and clubs, games and outdoor play, with full-day care on school breaks.

Key focus areas

  • Homework help and quiet study time
  • STEM challenges, arts and crafts
  • Sports, games and active outdoor play
  • Teamwork, character and responsibility
Ask about school-age openings

Frequently asked questions

What ages does Kinder Academy serve?

We care for children from 4 weeks old through 12 years, with infant, toddler, preschool, Pre-K and before & after school programs.

What are your hours?

We're open Monday through Friday from 6:00 AM to 7:00 PM, so families have flexibility for early starts and late commutes.

Are meals included in tuition?

Yes. Kinder Academy offers breakfast, lunch and an afternoon snack to all enrolled children.

Do you accept DES child care assistance?

Yes. We contract with the Arizona Department of Economic Security and accept Salt River Schools tribal scholarships to help income-eligible families access quality child care.

How do I enroll?

Start by scheduling a tour or requesting tuition rates above. Our director will walk you through availability, paperwork and your child's first-day transition plan.

Where are you located?

We're at 3259 W Baseline Rd in Laveen, AZ 85339, with easy drop-off and pick-up parking right out front.
